CandidateCensusComplete: FALSE

Purpose
Discover Reuters reporting of Ukrainian physical attacks against infrastructure and military/industrial targets in Russia proper, independently of the VIINA candidate list. The Reuters lane must be discovery-led; VIINA target names may be used only in a later reconciliation pass, not to define the initial Reuters search universe.

Search families
R1 ENERGY_REFINING: refinery, oil refinery, petrochemical plant, gas processing plant, fuel processing complex.
R2 ENERGY_STORAGE_EXPORT: oil depot, fuel depot, oil terminal, export terminal, port, loading berth, storage terminal.
R3 PIPELINE_PUMPING: pipeline, Druzhba, pumping station, compressor/pump infrastructure.
R4 DEFENSE_INDUSTRY: weapons factory, defence plant, ammunition components, explosives, UAV/drone factory, aircraft repair/manufacturing plant.
R5 STRATEGIC_AIR: air base, airfield, strategic bomber, military aircraft, fuel reservoirs associated with military air bases.
R6 GRID_SUPPORT: power plant, substation, electricity/heat infrastructure where the reported event may satisfy the DEEP target rules.
R7 LOGISTICS_OTHER: military logistics hubs, strategic rail/port nodes, other potentially census-relevant physical infrastructure.

Attack vocabulary
Ukraine, Ukrainian, SBU, GUR/HUR, General Staff, drone, UAV, missile, Neptune, strike, attack, explosion, fire, damaged, destroyed, shutdown, halted, suspended.

Temporal execution
Each R1-R7 family is searched across four fixed quarterly windows: Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, Oct-Dec 2025. Reuters chronology/summary articles are retained as discovery anchors, but each physical component is split to event/target level before dedupe. Search-engine results outside 2025 are rejected unless they explicitly provide retrospective evidence about a 2025 event; such retrospective evidence is confirmation only and cannot create a new 2025 Reuters raw hit without a 2025 Reuters source record.

Coding rules
- Split composite Reuters reports before dedupe.
- Use event date, not article publication date, as CanonicalEventDate.
- Follow-ups, repair reports and market-impact pieces link to the underlying physical event and are not additional events.
- Repeat attacks on the same node are distinct when Reuters establishes a fresh attack wave.
- Russia-proper only for the DEEP census; Crimea/Sevastopol and occupied Ukrainian territory remain audit exclusions.
- Reuters statements that merely repeat a Ukrainian claim without target-level physical-effect evidence remain CLAIMED/UNCONFIRMED until reconciled.
- Reuters reporting based on industry sources, regional authorities, operators, Reuters imagery or independently verified geolocation is retained with the appropriate confirmation status.
